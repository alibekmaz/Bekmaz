Project description

Limoo, a team communication platform tailored for the Iranian market, offers features such as chat, online meetings, file sharing, and tool integration via bots. Having successfully launched its MVP and attracted over 1,800 active users, Limoo is now aiming for expansion and is seeking new investors. To enhance its market appeal and usability, the company plans to refine its landing page design, aiming for a more delightful look that attracts both users and potential investors.
