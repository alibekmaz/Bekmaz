Project description

Gronard is a Germany based bicycle rack producer. They already had a website but they were not able to adjust it based on their needs whenever it was necessary. Therefore they wanted to have a website which is adjustable easily.

They requested us to develop something which is easy to adjust based on Shopware, which is a German open-source e-commerce platform.
